1. Product Ki Pehchan

Flipkart jaisa title likhiye: Brand, Model, Type aur Colour. Sirf CAPITAL letters ya kai emoji mat lagaiye.

🔗 Ek hi product ke alag color / size? Har variant ko alag product ki tarah add kijiye, bas niche ke 2 box mein same Variant Group likhiye. Customer ko product page par sab variants ek saath chune hue dikhenge. Sirf ek hi variant hai to ye khali chhod dijiye.

Us phone / product ke sabhi variants mein bilkul same likhiye.

Is product ka naam jo chip par dikhega. Har variant ka alag.

2. Product Photos

📸 Achhi photo ke niyam: asli product ki photo, saaf background, kam se kam 500 x 500 pixel. Doosri site ki photo ya watermark / phone number wali photo mat lagaiye. Pehli photo Main photo hoti hai: sabse pehle wahi dikhti hai. Alag-alag side (aage, peeche, side, andar ka) ki photos lagaiye.

Abhi koi photo nahi chuni.
