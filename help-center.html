Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I asked for OTP while logging in? ▼
PS Mart uses email OTP verification for login. Enter your registered email address and verify the OTP received in your email.
Where can I check my orders? ▼
Open your Profile and select My Orders to check your order information.
How can I get help with an order? ▼
If you have an issue with an order, contact PS Mart Support and provide your order details and problem.
What should I do if payment has a problem? ▼
First check your payment status and transaction details. If the issue continues, contact Support with the relevant payment information.
How can I ask a question about a product? ▼
Open the product page and use the Ask a Question option to submit your question.
Where can I check my support request? ▼
Open your Profile and select My Support Requests. There you can check whether your request is Pending, In Progress or Resolved.
