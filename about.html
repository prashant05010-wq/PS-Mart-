About PS Mart

Big Deals. Local Sellers. Better Shopping.

PS Mart is an online shopping and multi-vendor marketplace in India. We aim to connect customers with local and online sellers through one convenient marketplace.

Our Story

PS Mart began as an idea to make online shopping simpler and closer to the people who sell. Many local sellers have good products but find it hard to reach customers online, and many customers want one place where they can find those products.

PS Mart is our answer to that. It is an emerging marketplace where sellers can list their products and customers can browse, save favourites, add items to a cart and place orders.

PS Mart is a young brand and we are still growing. We will share our progress honestly as it happens.

Our Mission

To connect customers with local and online sellers through one convenient marketplace, and to make buying and selling online simple and reliable.

Our Vision

To grow PS Mart into a trusted Indian online marketplace where customers find what they need and sellers of every size can reach them.

Why PS Mart?

  • One marketplace, many sellersBrowse products from different sellers in one place instead of visiting many websites.
  • Room for local sellersLocal and online sellers can register and list their products on PS Mart.
  • Simple shopping toolsSearch by category, save items to a wishlist, use the cart and keep track of your orders.
  • Support when you need itCustomers and sellers can reach us through our contact page and help centre.

Our Marketplace Model

  1. Sellers joinSellers register on PS Mart and add their products through the seller dashboard.
  2. Customers shopCustomers browse categories, add products to the cart or wishlist and place orders.
  3. Orders are managedSellers manage their orders and products, and our team looks after support.
