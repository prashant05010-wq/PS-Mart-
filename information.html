💳 Payments & Payment Safety

For your security, never share your card number, CVV, PIN, OTP or banking password with another person. Payment information should only be entered through the official payment interface provided during checkout.

PS Mart does not ask users to share confidential banking credentials through chat, phone calls, social media messages or unofficial links.

🆘 Help & Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

Open the Products section, select a product, add it to your cart and continue through the available checkout process.

How can I manage my account?

Open Profile to update available personal information such as your name and phone number.

How can I ask a product question?

Open Questions & Answers or use the Ask a Question option available from supported product pages.

How can I write a product review?

Open the Reviews section to view and manage your product reviews.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious message?

Do not share OTPs, passwords, card details or other sensitive information. Use only official PS Mart pages and services.

How can I protect my account?

Keep your login credentials private and never share verification codes with anyone.
