Browser Help

Find answers to common questions about opening, using and troubleshooting PS Mart on mobile and desktop browsers.

Which browsers can I use to open PS Mart?

PS Mart is designed to work with modern web browsers such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox and Safari. For the best experience, keep your browser updated to the latest available version.

Can I use PS Mart on my mobile phone?

Yes. PS Mart is designed to work on smartphones and tablets. You can open the website through your mobile browser and use shopping, account and other available features.

Can I use PS Mart on a laptop or desktop?

Yes. PS Mart can also be accessed from a laptop or desktop computer using a supported modern browser.

The website is not loading. What should I do?

First check your internet connection. Then refresh the page. If the problem continues, close and reopen your browser or try opening PS Mart in another updated browser.

The page looks old or does not update. What should I do?

Your browser may be showing an older cached version of the page. Try refreshing the page. If necessary, clear the browser cache and open the website again.

Why am I being asked to log in again?

Your login session may have expired, your browser storage may have been cleared, or you may be using a different browser or device. Log in again using your registered account.

Why is OTP or email verification not working?

Check that you entered the correct email address and that your internet connection is working. Also check your spam or junk folder. If the verification message does not arrive, wait a short time and try again.

Why is my cart not showing correctly?

Cart information can depend on your browser's local storage. Make sure browser storage is enabled and avoid clearing website data while using the shopping cart.

What happens if I clear cookies or site data?

Clearing browser cookies or website data can remove stored preferences, login sessions and locally stored shopping information. You may need to log in again and restore some settings.

Why are images or products not appearing?

Check your internet connection and refresh the page. If images still do not appear, try another modern browser or clear the browser cache.

Why is PS Mart running slowly?

Slow internet, an outdated browser, many open browser tabs or heavy background activity can affect website performance. Try closing unnecessary tabs and refreshing the page.

Is it safe to use PS Mart on a public computer?

Avoid using sensitive account information on shared or public computers whenever possible. Always log out after using your account and do not save passwords or login information in a public browser.

Can I use private or incognito mode?

You can open PS Mart in private or incognito mode, but browser data may not be retained after the private session is closed. Some features that depend on stored browser data may therefore behave differently.

What should I do if a button or link does not work?

Refresh the page and try again. If the issue continues, try another supported browser or device. If the problem remains, contact PS Mart support with details about the page and feature that is not working.

How can I get the best PS Mart experience?

Use a stable internet connection, keep your browser updated, allow necessary website storage, and avoid using outdated browsers.
