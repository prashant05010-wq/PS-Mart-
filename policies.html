📋 PS Mart Policies

Important policies and guidelines for customers, sellers and users of the PS Mart platform.

These policies describe the general rules followed by PS Mart. Specific product, order or seller situations may also be subject to applicable laws and the terms shown during a transaction.

📚 Policy Directory

🔒 Privacy Policy How account and service information may be handled. 🛡️ Security Policy Account and platform security guidelines. 📦 Order Policy General rules related to orders and purchases. ❌ Cancellation Policy General information about order cancellation. 🔄 Return Policy General return-related information. 💰 Refund Policy General refund-related information. 🏪 Seller Policy Rules and responsibilities for sellers. ✍️ Content Policy Rules for reviews, questions and user content. 💳 Payment Safety Policy Important payment and credential safety information. 🚫 Prohibited Activities Activities that are not permitted on the platform.

1. 🔒 Privacy Policy

PS Mart may process information needed to provide account, shopping, seller and platform services.

  • Account information may be used to provide account-related services.
  • Information may be used to support shopping and platform functionality.
  • Users can manage available privacy preferences through the Privacy Center.
  • Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information through public areas.

For available privacy controls, visit the Privacy Center.

2. 🛡️ Security Policy

Users are expected to take reasonable steps to protect their PS Mart accounts and devices.

  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Never share PINs or CVVs with other people.
  • Do not enter credentials on suspicious websites.
  • Use only official PS Mart pages for account activities.
  • Report suspicious account activity through available support channels.

3. 📦 Order Policy

Orders are processed through the shopping and checkout features available on PS Mart.

  • Product availability can change.
  • Prices and product details should be checked before purchase.
  • An item in the cart is not necessarily a completed order.
  • Order confirmation depends on the applicable checkout process.
  • Delivery and fulfilment may depend on the seller and available services.

4. ❌ Cancellation Policy

Where cancellation functionality is available, an order may be cancelled according to the applicable order status and platform rules.

Cancellation availability can depend on whether the order has already been processed, shipped or otherwise fulfilled.

Any amount eligible for refund after cancellation will be handled according to the applicable payment and refund process.

5. 🔄 Return Policy

Returns may be available for eligible products where a return facility is offered.

  • Return eligibility may depend on the product and seller.
  • Return requests may be subject to applicable time limits.
  • Products may need to meet applicable return conditions.
  • Some products may not be eligible for return where permitted by applicable rules.

The applicable return information shown for a product or order should be reviewed before requesting a return.

6. 💰 Refund Policy

Refunds, where applicable, may depend on cancellation, return, payment status, order status and the applicable transaction rules.

  • A refund may not always be available for every transaction.
  • Refund processing time may depend on the payment method.
  • Third-party payment providers may have their own processing timelines.
  • Users should keep relevant order and transaction information for support.

7. 🏪 Seller Policy

Sellers are responsible for the products and information they provide through PS Mart.

  • Product information should be accurate and not intentionally misleading.
  • Sellers should comply with applicable laws.
  • Sellers should not knowingly list prohibited or unlawful products.
  • Product prices and availability should be maintained accurately.
  • Sellers should handle customer orders responsibly.
  • Sellers should not misuse customer information.
  • PS Mart may review seller activity for platform and security purposes.

8. ✍️ Content Policy

Users may submit reviews, questions, answers and feedback through supported PS Mart features.

  • Content should be relevant and truthful.
  • Do not post abusive or threatening content.
  • Do not publish private information belonging to another person.
  • Do not intentionally post fraudulent or misleading information.
  • Do not upload malicious code or harmful content.
  • Do not use content features for spam.

Content that violates applicable rules may be removed or restricted where appropriate.

9. 💳 Payment Safety Policy

PS Mart users should never share their OTP, CVV, PIN, password or banking credentials with another person.
  • Use only official checkout or payment interfaces.
  • Do not send card details through chat or social media.
  • Do not trust requests for OTPs from unknown people.
  • Check the website address before entering sensitive information.
  • Contact your bank or payment provider if you suspect unauthorized activity.

10. 🚫 Prohibited Activities

The following activities are not permitted when using PS Mart services:

  • Fraudulent transactions or scams.
  • Unauthorized access attempts.
  • Account impersonation.
  • Distribution of malware or harmful code.
  • Abuse of platform systems.
  • Attempts to bypass security controls.
  • Illegal use of the platform.
  • Misuse of customer, seller or other user information.

11. 👤 Customer Responsibility

  • Provide accurate account information.
  • Keep login credentials secure.
  • Review product and order information before purchase.
  • Use the platform responsibly.
  • Follow applicable laws and platform rules.
  • Do not misuse reviews, questions or other community features.

12. 🧑‍💼 Seller Responsibility

  • Provide genuine and accurate product information.
  • Maintain appropriate product listings.
  • Follow applicable consumer and business laws.
  • Protect customer information.
  • Handle orders and customer interactions responsibly.
  • Follow PS Mart seller requirements.

13. 🔄 Policy Changes

PS Mart may update these policies when required because of changes in services, security requirements, legal requirements or platform operations.

The latest version published on the PS Mart website should be considered when using the applicable service.

14. ⚖️ Legal Compliance

PS Mart and its users are expected to comply with applicable laws and regulations. Nothing on this page is intended to remove or restrict consumer rights that cannot legally be removed or restricted.

15. 🆘 Policy Support

If you have a question about a policy or an account-related matter, use the available PS Mart support and account features.

For privacy settings, visit the Privacy Center.

For general platform rules, see the Terms & Conditions.

📅 Policy Version

These policies are intended as the general PS Mart platform policy framework and may be supplemented by product-specific, seller-specific or transaction-specific rules where applicable.
