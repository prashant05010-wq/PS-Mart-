Withdraw ka paisa isi UPI ID par bheja jayega. UPI par naam wahi hona chahiye jo PAN par hai.

Seller ke niyam (sankshep):
• Sirf asli aur sahi vivaran wale products bechiye. Nakli ya galat jaankari par account band ho sakta hai.
• Har bikri par PS Mart ka commission lagta hai. Commission % admin dashboard se tay hota hai.
• Customer ko deliver hone ke 7 din tak return ka haq hai.
• Deliver hone ke 7 din baad hi paisa aapke wallet mein judta hai, jise aap withdraw kar sakte hain.
• Aapka account admin ki approval ke baad hi chalu hoga.
